## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: Grounded, not templated — right-to-work fact line, first-person annotation, and CTA/link ordering trace to this candidate's real story. But the composition's signature device — the hand-drawn arrow meant to connect the margin note to "full-stack" in the headline — doesn't do that job as built.

Deterministic scan: `impeccable detect --json` on Header.tsx, Hero.tsx, App.tsx returned [], exit 0 — clean. Expected: the detector catches markup-level slop patterns, not compositional judgment calls like whether an arrow points at what it claims to.

Visual overlays: No in-page live-server overlay was injected; evidence came from direct Playwright screenshots and DOM queries instead.

## Overall Impression

Strong content decisions, clean execution of the "quiet notebook" tone, genuinely working accessibility fundamentals (contrast, reduced-motion, skip link). The biggest miss is structural: the one motif this design system is built around — an annotation physically pointing at something — was built as two components sitting near each other rather than one that actually points.

## What's Working

- The fact line (London, UK · Right to work in the UK · Graduate Visa to Sep 2027) answers the first question a UK recruiter has, styled as a fact (mono) not marketing copy.
- Contrast measured, not assumed: ink-muted/paper 6.90:1, accent/paper 6.28:1 — both clear WCAG AA's 4.5:1 with margin.
- useReducedMotion is actually wired up — pathLength animation skipped and rendered at full length instantly when the OS preference is set; no console errors on load.

## Priority Issues

[P0] identity.rightToWork exists but Hero doesn't use it — two sources of truth for a legally sensitive fact
- Why it matters: content.ts line 24 defines rightToWork; Hero.tsx's fact line hardcodes a different literal string. Visa/right-to-work status is the one fact here a wrong edit could actually hurt.
- Fix: Derive the fact line from identity.rightToWork.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

[P1] The signature annotation arrow doesn't point at anything
- Why it matters: Confirmed in both screenshots — the arrow curves toward the annotation paragraph beside it, never toward the accent-colored "full-stack" word in the headline it's supposed to annotate.
- Fix: Reposition the arrow to originate from/near "full-stack" in the h1, or stop claiming the connection.
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout

[P1] CTA order and weight contradict the stated "proof-first" goal
- Why it matters: "Email me" is primary (heavier underline), "View work" secondary — backwards for a portfolio whose success metric is callbacks earned by demonstrated craft.
- Fix: Swap visual weight/order — View work primary, Email me secondary.
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout

[P2] Touch targets well under 44x44
- Why it matters: Measured at 375px: nav links 14px tall, hero CTAs 24px tall, GitHub/LinkedIn 16px tall, no padding around text.
- Fix: Add vertical padding to enlarge hit areas without changing visible text size.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

[P2] No <main> landmark
- Why it matters: document.querySelector('main') returns false. Skip link targets #main on a <section>, not an actual <main> element — screen-reader landmark navigation has nothing to jump to.
- Fix: Wrap page content in <main id="main"> in App.tsx.
- Suggested command: /impeccable audit
